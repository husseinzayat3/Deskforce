# Custom builds

Deskforce is free and open source, and you can use it as it is. If you want it shaped around your company or your clients, I build customised versions. (For AI, backend or leadership work beyond Deskforce, see [Work with me](work-with-me.md).)

## What I can build for you

- **Your team and workflow** — your own roles, instructions, approval steps and reporting.
- **Your tools** — integrations with the systems you already use: ticketing (Jira, Linear), your git host (GitHub, GitLab), CRM, chat (Teams, Discord), internal APIs.
- **Your models** — other AI providers (Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock, OpenRouter, Mistral…), private or self-hosted models, your own pricing and spending rules.
- **Your brand** — a custom floorplan, avatars, name and look, packaged as your own desktop app.
- **Your infrastructure** — a hosted, multi-user version with logins and permissions, deployed where you need it.

## How it works

1. **Tell me what you need** — the team you have in mind, the tools it should use, where it should run.
2. **I reply with a proposal** — what I will build, how long it takes, and the price.
3. **I build it, and you try it** as it takes shape.
4. **Hand-over** — the code, instructions to run it, and support while you get started.
