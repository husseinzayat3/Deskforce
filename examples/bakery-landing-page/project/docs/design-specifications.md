# Crumb & Co Landing Page Design Specifications

## Layout
- **Header:** Sticky, remains at the top as users scroll.
- **Hero Section:** Full-width with a welcoming message and visuals to capture the bakery's vibe.
- **Menu Section:** Below the hero section, showcasing the bakery's menu with item names and prices.
- **Opening Hours Section:** Following the menu, clearly displaying the bakery's operating hours.
- **Contact Form Section:** Positioned towards the bottom, providing an easy way for customers to reach out.
- **Footer:** Includes the year 2026.

## Font Pairing
- **Primary Font:** "Montserrat", for headings and key text elements.
- **Secondary Font:** "Roboto", for body text and longer passages.

## Colour Palette
- **Primary Colour:** Warm Orange (#FFA500) for buttons and highlights.
- **Secondary Colour:** Soft Cream (#FFF4E1) for backgrounds.
- **Accent Colour:** Dark Chocolate (#8B4513) for text and borders.
- **Gradient:** Orange to Cream gradient for section headers.

## CSS Gradients
- Use a linear gradient from Warm Orange (#FFA500) to Soft Cream (#FFF4E1) on section headers to provide depth and visual interest.

## Visuals
- Utilize emojis such as 🥖, 🍞, and 🥐 for menus and to add charm and character.
- Inline SVG for simple icons like a phone for contact.
- CSS shapes for decorative elements such as circles or waves.

These specifications will ensure a cohesive, warm, and inviting design for the "Crumb & Co" bakery landing page, delivering a modern yet friendly experience for users.
